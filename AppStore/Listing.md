**Promotional text** (162/170)

Your room is full of invisible radio. Aether turns the Bluetooth signals around you into slow, drifting ambient music. Walk around and the music changes with you.

**Description** (1605/4000)

```
Your room is full of invisible radio: phones, watches, earbuds, speakers and TVs, all quietly broadcasting several times a second. Aether listens to that field and plays it as ambient music.

Every device it hears becomes a voice with its own short melody on its own slow loop. No two loops share a length, so the melodies drift in and out of step and never quite repeat, in the spirit of Brian Eno's Music for Airports. A soft pad chord moves beneath them.

THE ROOM IS THE INSTRUMENT
• Walk toward a device and its voice grows louder and brighter, unfolding the rest of its phrase.
• Cup the phone in your hands and the music dims. Your body is mostly water, which absorbs radio.
• An unsteady signal makes its notes waver like a warped tape.
• Turn around or walk through a doorway and the whole chord reshapes: radio waves are only about 12 cm long, so the field changes from step to step.

SHAPE THE SOUND
• 13 scales and modes, any key, octave shift
• Up to 32 voices for crowded rooms
• Response from slow drift to instant snap, so you can demonstrate the field or simply listen
• A live radar of every emitter around you
• AirPlay to any speaker

DUO WITH BELLWEATHERED
Open Bellweathered on a second iPhone nearby and the two find each other. The sky sets the key, the room sets the tempo, and they play together.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your iPhone. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing about the devices around you is stored or sent anywhere.

Headphones recommended.
```

**What's New:** First release.

**App Privacy:** "Data Not Collected". (Bluetooth signals are processed on device and never leave it; Duo messages go only to the paired phone.)

**Age rating:** answer "None" / "No" to every question → 4+.

**App Review notes** (App Review Information › Notes; no sign-in required):

```
Aether is a musical instrument. Tap "Listen" on the main screen to start the sound; it makes no sound until then. It reads the signal strength of nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements (with permission) and turns each one into a melodic voice. With a few Bluetooth devices nearby (an Apple Watch, AirPods, another phone), you will hear several voices; moving the phone or covering it with your hands changes the sound. The radar shows each device.

Bluetooth data is processed on device only and never stored or transmitted.

The optional "Duo" feature pairs with our companion app Bellweathered on a second nearby iPhone via MultipeerConnectivity (Local Network permission). It is not needed to review the app; Aether is fully functional on its own. Duo can be turned off in Settings > Duo.
```

**Promotional text** (138/170)

Hear the weather. Bellweathered turns the sky where you are, its clouds, wind, rain, sun and moon, into a slowly changing drone with bells.

**Description** (1475/4000)

```
Bellweathered plays the sky where you are.

It reads the live weather for your location and works out where the sun and moon are, then turns it all into a slowly changing ambient piece: a deep drone that brightens at dawn and darkens at night, wind that gusts when the wind does, the hiss of rain or the hush of snow, and seven bell-like melodies drifting on long, unequal loops.

WHAT YOU HEAR
• Daylight lifts the upper voices; night deepens the low ones
• Cloud dulls the tone and deepens the reverb; humidity softens it
• Wind sets the gusts and how fast the drone breathes
• Rain makes the melodies busier and louder; snow makes them soft and slow
• Low pressure stretches the drone wider
• A bell strikes on every solar hour
• The full moon pulses gently underneath

WHAT YOU SEE
An animated sky that follows the real one: sun and moon in their places, the moon in its phase, stars at night, drifting clouds, rain or snow, and a ripple for every note.

CHOOSE YOUR SOUND
Nine scales, any key, three melody voices (glass bell, string, kalimba), and a simulated sky when you're offline or just want to wander through the weather.

DUO WITH AETHER
Open Aether on a second iPhone nearby and the two play together: the sky sets the key, and the radio room sets the tempo.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your approximate location is used only to fetch the weather and show your town's name.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. Headphones recommended.
```

**What's New:** First release.

**App Privacy:** Data Collected → **Location › Coarse Location**. Purpose: **App Functionality**. Linked to the user: **No**. Used for tracking: **No**. (Rounded coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the weather; Apple's geocoder makes the town name.)

**Age rating:** answer "None" / "No" to every question → 4+.

**App Review notes** (App Review Information › Notes; no sign-in required):

```
Bellweathered is an ambient music app. Tap "Begin" to start the sound; it makes no sound until then. It uses approximate location (when in use) to fetch the current weather from Open-Meteo and to place the sun and moon. If location is denied it uses a default place, and if the network is unavailable it runs a simulated sky; either way it is fully playable. Settings lets you switch to "Simulated" weather to hear different conditions quickly.

The optional "Duo" feature pairs with our companion app Aether on a second nearby iPhone via MultipeerConnectivity (Local Network permission). It is not needed to review the app. Duo can be turned off in Settings > Duo.
```

## Before you upload

- Set each target's Team to your **paid** team (not "Personal Team"). If Xcode says a bundle ID is unavailable, add a suffix (e.g. `com.samuelling.aether.app`).
- Version is 1.0, Build 1. Every later upload needs a higher Build number.
- Export compliance is pre-answered (no non-exempt encryption).
- EU: App Store Connect asks for your Digital Services Act trader status. Whether you count as a trader is your call; a hobbyist publishing free apps with no business behind them would typically choose non-trader.
- Turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings › Pages › Deploy from branch › main › /docs) so the privacy and support URLs above go live.
