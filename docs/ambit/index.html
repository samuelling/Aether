Ambit

Four instruments that play the invisible world around you, together, in one key and one tempo.

Aether · the radio room

The Bluetooth signals around you, each one a voice on its own slow loop. Aether sets the tempo and the chords.

Bellweathered · the weather

The live weather where you are, as a deep, pure bass drone with wind, rain and an hourly bell. It sets the key.

Leitmotif · your phone

The rhythm of your iPhone's own data traffic (never its content), sung as short piano motifs.

Orbitakt · the satellites

The satellites overhead, played as sparse dub percussion by a radar beam sweeping your sky.

Tips

  • No sound? Tap Play on the main screen, and check the ring/silent switch and volume.
  • Only one instrument playing? The puck on the mix pad is pushed to an edge. Double-tap the pad to centre it.
  • Aether hears nothing? Allow Bluetooth in Settings › Ambit. iOS only lets apps hear Bluetooth while they're on screen, so in the background Aether keeps playing the last room it heard.
  • Simulated sky? Ambit couldn't reach the weather or satellite data, or your location. It switches to the live sky when it can.
  • Crackles? Settings › Audio load shows how hard each instrument is working. Turning one off, or pushing the puck away from it, frees up room.
